While reviewing the login endpoint for our vendor assessment, we noticed that a
wrong password for a real account takes about 250 ms to be refused, while an
address with no account is refused in under 10 ms. The error message is
identical in both cases, which is good, but the timing alone is enough to test a
list of addresses.
