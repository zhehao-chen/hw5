When I duplicate a report whose name is close to the 120-character limit, the
copy's name ends in `...` instead of `(copy)`, and everything before that is
identical to the original. In the list the two look the same, and I've edited
the wrong one twice. Shorter names get ` (copy)` at the end as expected.
