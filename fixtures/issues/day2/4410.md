On our own instance we set `TOKEN_TTL_MINUTES=12h` so that people stay signed in
for a working day. Since then everyone is sent back to the login page about
twelve minutes after logging in, and the login response says
`expiresInMinutes: 12`. The service started without any complaint about the
value.
