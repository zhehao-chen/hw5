The weekly data check found 41 accounts with categories that differ only by
spaces at the end, such as `Travel` and `Travel `. Reports filtered on `Travel`
leave out the rows stored as `Travel `, so their totals come out lower than the
account holder expects. Descriptions are tidied up on import, but the category
is stored exactly as it was in the file.
