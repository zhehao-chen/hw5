The weekly accessibility check found that error messages shown as toasts use the
same polite live region as success messages, so screen readers wait before
announcing them. They also disappear after five seconds, like every other toast,
so a user who is slow to reach the message never sees it. Errors should be
announced at once and stay until they are dismissed.
