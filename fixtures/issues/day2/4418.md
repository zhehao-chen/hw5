The nightly reconciliation check compares imported amounts with the source file.
For an account in Kuwaiti dinar, 23 of 140 transactions imported on 20 October
differ: `12.346` in the file is stored and exported as `12.35`, and `0.125` as
`0.13`. KWD, BHD, OMR and JOD have three decimal places, and amounts in them are
being rounded to two.
