While testing our integration I created a report, by mistake, with an
`accountId` belonging to a different test user. The API accepted it with 201,
and exporting the report returned that other user's transactions. Uploads do
check that the account is yours (they answer `account not found`), but creating
or editing a report doesn't seem to. Account ids are UUIDs, so one has to leak
first, but they do turn up in storage paths and support tickets.
