During last Thursday's mail relay outage, the 'Forgot password' form showed a
server error for every real account we tried, but the usual 'check your email'
answer for addresses that have no account. For about an hour, anyone could tell
which addresses are registered just by trying them. Once the relay came back,
every address got the same answer again.
