A check of last week's scheduled export attachments found 37 file names like
`intercompany-transfers-between-the-benelux-and-nordic-firms--2026-10-21.xlsx`.
All are for reports with long names: the name is cut to 60 characters, and when
the cut falls just after a space or a comma, a hyphen is left at the end before
the date is added. Reports with shorter names are not affected.
