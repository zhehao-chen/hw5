Relay logs for 27 October show 61 messages refused with permanent errors
(`550 5.1.1 Recipient address rejected` and
`552 5.3.4 Message size exceeds fixed maximum`), and each one was submitted
three times within about two seconds. A 5xx reply will not change on a retry, so
this triples the load on the relay and has put our sending domain on its watch
list. Temporary 4xx replies should still be retried.
